# External activation protocol

Goal: observe 12 consenting external users, including at least 6 with no prior
relationship to the maintainer, from invitation to possible second use. This
file contains no participants or results yet.

## Procedure

1. Recruit 6 or more cold participants through individual, relevant outreach;
   recruit the remainder from existing contacts. Do not post or send anything
   until outreach is separately authorized.
2. Send only the public README. Do not coach unless the participant asks; log
   every hint because it changes the activation result.
3. Start the timer when the participant opens the install instructions. Stop
   it when the first real `review_plan` result appears, or record abandonment.
4. Record the result as useful finding, no useful addition, operational
   failure, or abandoned. Keep the participant's wording in the notes.
5. Seven days later, ask whether a second real review occurred. Do not infer a
   second use from an install, star, or stated intention.

Success for one participant means a real review in under 10 minutes. Report
raw counts and times only after data exist; do not claim statistical validity.

## Approach messages

Cold outreach:

> I am testing whether a small open-source checkpoint for Claude Code plans is
> understandable without coaching. Would you be willing to try the public
> install and one real plan review while I time the path? I want to learn
> whether it can be completed in under ten minutes; that is the test, not a
> promise. I will record only a pseudonymous source category, elapsed time,
> result, abandonment reason, and whether you use it again within seven days.
> No posting or endorsement is requested.

Existing contact:

> Could you test Inspectrum from its public README and run one real plan review?
> I am measuring time to first result, what blocked you, and whether you use it
> a second time within seven days. Please avoid secrets in the plan. I will not
> quote or identify you without separate permission.
